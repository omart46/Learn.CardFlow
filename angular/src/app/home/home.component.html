YOUR OPERATIONAL FLOW

From request to handover.

Six stages. Clear responsibilities.
01

Enrollment

Create employee card requests. Correct rejected details and resubmit for review.

02

Review & decision

Reviewers start the review. Supervisors approve requests or return them with a rejection reason.

03

Build a batch

Approved requests appear in Ready for Production. Add them to a draft batch without copying IDs.

04

Production

Start production to lock the batch contents, then mark the batch completed when finished.

05

Quality check

Inspect produced cards. Record a pass or a failure with a clear reason.

06

Delivery

Hand over passed cards and record the recipient, delivery time, and delivery officer.
